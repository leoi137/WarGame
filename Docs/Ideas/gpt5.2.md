# The “something else” that will make the game amazing (high impact systems)

## 1) Seasonal Levy & Campaign Calendar (must-have)

Most 1016 armies are not permanent standing armies. Add:

* **Levy armies decay after X turns** (harvest season)
* **Winter attrition** in north/east
* **Monsoon penalties** in India/SE Asia
* **Sailing seasons** for Viking/Srivijaya/Chola naval campaigns

This instantly makes medieval warfare feel medieval.

## 2) Trade Route Warfare Layer

Add **trade nodes + convoys**:

* North Sea
* Baltic river routes
* Silk Road oases
* Nile
* Persian Gulf
* Bay of Bengal
* Strait of Malacca

Armies should be expensive; trade funds elites. Raiding trade becomes a strategy, not just city spam.

## 3) Legitimacy / Religion / Revolt Meter

Cnut, Basil II, Fatimids, Abbasids, Song frontier states—all benefit from legitimacy mechanics.

* Low legitimacy → levy efficiency drops, revolts rise
* High legitimacy → faster musters, better tax income, allied vassals hold longer

## 4) Special “Great Captain” units (optional but awesome)

Each major faction gets 1–2 named commander slots:

* Cnut, Basil II, Rajendra Chola, Mahmud of Ghazni, etc.
* Commanders grant formation bonuses (river crossing, cavalry shock, siege speed, naval interception)

# Fast balance conversion (historical estimate → WC4 unit cap)

If you’re using a WC4-like scale, don’t spawn all estimated troops. Use a conversion:

* **1 game infantry unit = ~2,500–5,000 real troops**
* **1 cavalry unit = ~1,000–2,000**
* **1 navy unit = flotilla/squadron, not single ship**
* **Map-start forces = 15–25% of estimated total military**
* **Mobilizable reserve = 40–60%**
* **Emergency levy = rest (lower quality)**

That gives you believable openings without 900,000 Song troops flooding the map on turn 1.
